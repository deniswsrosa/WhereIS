## Africa (46)

### 🇩🇿 Algeria
- It is the largest country in Africa, and the Sahara Desert blankets roughly 80% of its territory.
- It holds some of the world's largest natural gas reserves, piped beneath the Mediterranean to Europe.
- The Hoggar Mountains rise from the deep south to jagged volcanic peaks near Tamanrasset.
- The Tuareg, veiled in indigo, are known as the 'blue people' of its southern desert.
- Couscous, recognized by UNESCO, sits at the heart of its cuisine.

### 🇦🇴 Angola
- Oil and diamonds make it one of Africa's leading resource economies.
- Its arid south is home to the welwitschia, a desert plant that can live over 1,000 years.
- Angola is the second-largest Portuguese-speaking country in the world by area, after Brazil.
- The Cabinda exclave is cut off from the rest of the country by a strip of the DR Congo.
- Semba, a lively musical genre, is widely regarded as a precursor to Brazilian samba.
- Funge, a stiff porridge made from cassava or maize flour, is a staple at Angolan tables.
- The cold Atlantic coast of the far south gives way to the dunes of the Namib Desert.
- Agostinho Neto, a poet and physician, became the country's first president upon independence in 1975.

### 🇧🇯 Benin
- It is the birthplace of Vodun (Voodoo), still practiced openly as an official religion.
- The Kingdom of Dahomey fielded the Agojie, an elite all-female regiment of warriors.
- Ganvie is a village of stilt houses on a lagoon, nicknamed the "Venice of Africa."
- Ouidah's "Door of No Return" memorializes a major departure point of the slave trade.
- The Royal Palaces of Abomey are a UNESCO World Heritage site of the old kingdom.
- Cotonou's sprawling Dantokpa is one of West Africa's largest open-air markets.
- It is one of West Africa's leading producers of cotton.
- Porto-Novo is the capital, though Cotonou is the largest city.

### 🇧🇼 Botswana
- The Okavango Delta is one of the world's largest inland deltas, flooding the Kalahari each year.
- It is home to the largest elephant population on Earth, over 130,000 animals.
- The Makgadikgadi salt pans are so vast they are visible from space.
- Diamond discoveries transformed it from one of the poorest nations into a middle-income success.
- The Tsodilo Hills hold thousands of ancient rock paintings, the "Louvre of the Desert."

### 🇧🇫 Burkina Faso
- Its name translates roughly to "Land of Upright (Incorruptible) People."
- It is one of the world's leading gold producers, now its top export.
- Cotton, once called 'white gold,' remains a pillar of its farming economy.
- The ruins of Loropéni, a mysterious stone fortress, are its first UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Its capital, Ouagadougou, is affectionately shortened by locals to 'Ouaga.'

### 🇧🇮 Burundi
- It is one of the most densely populated countries in mainland Africa.
- Cattle hold deep cultural value here, long a traditional symbol of status and respect.
- Gitega was restored as the country's political capital in 2019.
- Kirundi, spoken by nearly everyone, unites the nation as a shared mother tongue.
- Rusizi National Park protects hippos and crocodiles where the river meets the plain.

### 🇨🇻 Cabo Verde
- It is an archipelago of ten volcanic islands, nine of them inhabited.
- It served for centuries as an Atlantic hub of Portuguese trade before independence in 1975.
- Sal and Boa Vista draw sun-seekers to long white beaches and steady trade winds.
- Its diaspora abroad outnumbers the population living on the islands themselves.
- The flat island of Sal was named for the salt once harvested from its crater pans.

### 🇨🇲 Cameroon
- It is nicknamed "Africa in miniature" for packing in deserts, rainforests, mountains and coast.
- The far-north Waza reserve is among the region's finest for spotting lions and giraffes.
- Its Baka communities are among Central Africa's forest-dwelling hunter-gatherer peoples.
- The colonial-era Bimbia site marks a departure point of the transatlantic slave trade.
- Its Bamileke and Bamoun kingdoms are known for elaborate beaded masks and royal thrones.

### 🇨🇫 Central African Republic
- The Dzanga-Sangha reserve hosts forest elephants and western lowland gorillas.
- It is among the least-visited countries on the planet.
- Boali Falls plunges dramatically on the Mbari River near the capital.
- It holds significant diamond and uranium deposits.
- The Sangha river basin is part of one of the world's great intact rainforests.

### 🇹🇩 Chad
- Lake Chad, once one of Africa's largest lakes, has shrunk by roughly 90% since the 1960s.
- The Ennedi Plateau is a maze of sandstone arches, towers and ancient rock art.
- Dust from its Bodélé Depression fertilizes the Amazon rainforest across the Atlantic.
- "Toumaï," one of the oldest known hominid fossils, was discovered here.
- Zakouma National Park staged a remarkable comeback of its elephant herds.

### 🇨🇬 Congo (Republic of the)
- It sits within the Congo Basin, the world's second-largest tropical rainforest.
- It is often called "Congo-Brazzaville" to distinguish it from its larger neighbour.
- Odzala-Kokoua National Park is a stronghold for western lowland gorillas.
- The Congo River, the world's deepest, traces much of its southern border.
- Congolese rumba, born along its river, is recognized by UNESCO.

### 🇨🇩 DR Congo
- It is the second-largest country in Africa by area.
- The Congo River is the world's second-largest by water discharge and the deepest anywhere.
- It is the only place where the okapi, a striped relative of the giraffe, lives in the wild.
- Mount Nyiragongo holds one of the world's largest and most active lava lakes.
- Its mines produce the majority of the world's cobalt, vital for batteries.

### 🇨🇮 Côte d'Ivoire
- It is the world's largest producer of cocoa beans.
- Yamoussoukro's Basilica of Our Lady of Peace is the largest church building on Earth.
- Its official name is used only in French, and it asks not to be translated.
- Abidjan is one of West Africa's most modern skyline cities.
- Its name means "Ivory Coast," from the old ivory trade.

### 🇩🇯 Djibouti
- Lake Assal is the lowest point in Africa and one of the saltiest bodies of water on Earth.
- It guards the Bab-el-Mandeb strait, a chokepoint for global shipping.
- Whale sharks gather seasonally in its Gulf of Tadjoura.
- The Ardoukôba area sits on a spreading rift where the continent is slowly tearing apart.
- Lake Abbe's eerie limestone chimneys look like an alien landscape.

### 🇬🇶 Equatorial Guinea
- It is the only sovereign African country with Spanish as an official language.
- It is building a brand-new planned capital, Ciudad de la Paz, in the interior jungle.
- It is made up of a mainland region plus several islands in the Gulf of Guinea.
- Portuguese and French stand alongside Spanish as official languages.
- The remote volcanic island of Annobón lies far to the south, below the equator.

### 🇪🇷 Eritrea
- It is one of the most closed and tightly controlled countries in the world.
- Its Danakil coast reaches some of the hottest inhabited temperatures on Earth.
- Its capital, Asmara, sits on a high plateau nearly 2,300 metres above the Red Sea.
- Vintage Italian espresso culture lingers in its cafés from colonial days.
- It runs one of the longest and most sweeping national service programs in the world.

### 🇸🇿 Eswatini
- It was renamed from Swaziland in 2018 on its 50th independence anniversary.
- It is one of the world's last remaining absolute monarchies.
- The annual Umhlanga (Reed Dance) is a spectacular cultural gathering.
- Sibebe Rock is one of the largest exposed granite domes on the planet.
- It is a tiny landlocked kingdom almost entirely surrounded by South Africa.

### 🇪🇹 Ethiopia
- It was never colonized, defeating an Italian invasion at the Battle of Adwa in 1896.
- It keeps its own calendar of 13 months and a clock that starts the day at dawn.
- The rock-hewn churches of Lalibela were carved downward out of solid stone.
- "Lucy," a 3.2-million-year-old hominid skeleton, was unearthed here.
- It is widely regarded as the birthplace of coffee.

### 🇬🇦 Gabon
- Nearly 88% of the country is covered in rainforest.
- At Loango, elephants, gorillas and hippos wander onto the Atlantic beaches — even surfing hippos.
- The equator runs directly through it.
- It set aside 13 national parks at once in 2002 to protect its forests.
- Oil has long funded one of Central Africa's higher per-capita incomes.

### 🇬🇲 Gambia
- It is the smallest country on mainland Africa, a thin ribbon around a river.
- It is almost entirely surrounded by Senegal, following the Gambia River inland.
- It is nicknamed the "Smiling Coast of Africa."
- Kunta Kinteh Island is tied to the story behind Alex Haley's "Roots."
- It is a renowned birdwatching destination with hundreds of species.

### 🇬🇭 Ghana
- Colourful kente cloth originated with its Ashanti and Ewe peoples.
- Its old name, the "Gold Coast," reflected its rich gold trade.
- It was the first sub-Saharan African country to win independence, in 1957.
- The slave castles of Cape Coast and Elmina are sombre UNESCO memorials.
- Lake Volta is one of the world's largest reservoirs by surface area.
- Kwame Nkrumah, its founding leader, was a giant of pan-Africanism.
- Its Black Stars are among Africa's most beloved national football teams.
- Jollof rice is a fiercely debated staple across West Africa.

### 🇬🇳 Guinea
- The griot tradition of praise-singers and oral historians runs deep in its culture.
- Sékou Touré served as the nation's first president for more than a quarter of a century.
- The singer Mory Kanté reached global fame with his electrifying hit Yé ké yé ké.
- The Îles de Los form a small tropical archipelago just off the coast near Conakry.
- Fula, Malinké, and Susu number among the country's principal spoken languages.
- Chimpanzees in its forests are famous for cracking nuts by using stones as tools.
- The territory was known as French Guinea throughout the colonial period.
- The sprawling Madina Market in Conakry ranks among West Africa's busiest trading hubs.

### 🇬🇼 Guinea-Bissau
- Cashew nuts are the backbone of its economy.
- Some Bijagós islands preserve matriarchal traditions where women choose husbands.
- It won independence from Portugal in 1974 after a long liberation war.
- Carnival in Bissau bursts with vivid masks rooted in local traditions.
- Its offshore waters are among West Africa's richest fishing grounds.

### 🇱🇸 Lesotho
- It is the only country entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation — the "Kingdom in the Sky."
- It has the highest low point of any country on Earth.
- Afriski is one of the few ski resorts in Southern Africa.
- Basotho blankets and hardy mountain ponies are cultural icons.
- It is one of only three nations completely surrounded by a single other country.

### 🇱🇷 Liberia
- It was founded in the 1800s as a home for freed African-American slaves.
- Its flag and capital, Monrovia (named for a US president), echo the United States.
- Sapo National Park protects one of West Africa's last primary rainforests.
- It elected Africa's first female head of state, Ellen Johnson Sirleaf.
- It is one of only two African countries never formally colonized by Europe.

### 🇱🇾 Libya
- Leptis Magna and Sabratha are spectacular, sprawling Roman ruins on its coast.
- The Sahara covers almost the entire country.
- The Great Man-Made River pipes ancient aquifer water across the desert to the coast.
- It has the longest Mediterranean coastline of any African nation.
- Waw an Namus is a black volcanic crater ringed by tiny colored lakes deep in the Sahara.

### 🇲🇬 Madagascar
- Around 90% of its wildlife exists nowhere else on Earth.
- It is the only place where lemurs live in the wild.
- The Avenue of the Baobabs lines the road with towering, ancient trees.
- The Tsingy de Bemaraha is a razor-sharp "forest" of limestone pinnacles.
- It split from the African landmass around 160 million years ago.

### 🇲🇼 Malawi
- Lake Malawi holds more fish species than any other lake in the world.
- It is nicknamed the "Warm Heart of Africa" for its friendly people.
- Its colourful cichlid fish are prized in aquariums worldwide.
- Mount Mulanje rises abruptly as a granite massif from the plains.
- Explorer David Livingstone helped make the lake known to Europe.

### 🇲🇷 Mauritania
- The Richat Structure, or "Eye of the Sahara," is a giant bullseye visible from space.
- Chinguetti's ancient desert libraries preserve centuries-old Islamic manuscripts.
- One of the world's longest trains hauls iron ore across the desert to the coast.
- It was the last country in the world to officially abolish slavery, in 1981.
- Banc d'Arguin is a crucial stopover for millions of migratory birds.

### 🇲🇿 Mozambique
- It is the only single-word country name containing all five vowels.
- Its flag is the only national flag featuring a modern rifle, the AK-47.
- The Bazaruto Archipelago shelters some of the last dugongs in the region.
- Ilha de Moçambique is a tiny UNESCO-listed island of Swahili and Portuguese history.
- Its long Indian Ocean coastline is fringed with coral reefs.

### 🇳🇦 Namibia
- Sossusvlei's red dunes are among the tallest in the world, some over 300 metres.
- The Namib is considered the oldest desert on the planet.
- Deadvlei is a haunting clay pan of blackened, 900-year-old dead trees.
- The Skeleton Coast is littered with shipwrecks and whale bones.
- It is one of the least densely populated countries on Earth.

### 🇳🇪 Niger
- The last wild herds of the endangered West African giraffe survive here.
- It takes its name from the Niger River, which sweeps through its southwest.
- The Wodaabe people hold the Gerewol, a courtship festival judged on male beauty.
- Its deserts are rich in dinosaur fossils, including the long-necked Nigersaurus.
- It is one of the hottest countries on Earth, largely blanketed by the Sahara.

### 🇳🇬 Nigeria
- It is the most populous country in Africa, with over 220 million people.
- Nollywood is one of the largest film industries in the world by output.
- It is Africa's largest economy and one of the world's major oil exporters.
- Its Afrobeats stars have become some of the biggest names in global pop.
- The ancient Nok culture shaped striking terracotta figures over 2,000 years ago.

### 🇸🇹 Sao Tome and Principe
- It is the second-smallest country in Africa, made of two volcanic islands.
- Its isolation gave it many unique bird and plant species.
- Portuguese is the official language of this former Portuguese colony.
- Príncipe island is a UNESCO biosphere reserve cloaked in dense rainforest.
- It won its independence from Portugal in 1975.

### 🇸🇳 Senegal
- Lake Retba, the "Pink Lake," glows rose-colored from salt-loving algae.
- Gorée Island is a moving memorial to the transatlantic slave trade.
- The African Renaissance Monument in Dakar stands taller than the Statue of Liberty.
- The famous Dakar Rally took its name from finishing here.
- Its culture of "teranga" (hospitality) is a point of national pride.

### 🇸🇨 Seychelles
- The coco de mer palm produces the largest and heaviest seed in the plant kingdom.
- Aldabra Atoll hosts over 100,000 giant tortoises, more than the Galápagos.
- Its inner islands are among the oldest oceanic granite islands on Earth.
- It has one of the highest incomes per person in Africa.
- The Vallée de Mai is said to have inspired legends of the Garden of Eden.

### 🇸🇱 Sierra Leone
- Its name means "Lion Mountains," from the peninsula's peaks.
- Freetown was founded as a home for freed and rescued slaves.
- The Tacugama sanctuary protects orphaned chimpanzees.
- Its capital is one of the rainiest in the world.
- It sits atop significant diamond deposits, including some record-breaking finds.

### 🇸🇴 Somalia
- It sits on the Horn of Africa, the continent's easternmost tip.
- Nearly the whole population shares one language, Somali, and a common heritage.
- Mogadishu, its coastal capital, was a thriving medieval Indian Ocean trading port.
- Its deep love of verse has earned it a reputation as a 'nation of poets.'
- The self-declared republic of Somaliland in the north runs its own government.

### 🇸🇸 South Sudan
- It is the world's youngest country, gaining independence in 2011.
- It is home to more than 60 distinct ethnic groups and languages.
- English was chosen as its official language at independence.
- Oil provides the overwhelming majority of its government revenue.
- Juba, its riverside capital, is one of the world's newest national capitals.

### 🇸🇩 Sudan
- It has more ancient pyramids than Egypt, clustered at Meroë.
- Powerful Nubian ("Black Pharaoh") kingdoms once ruled all the way into Egypt.
- It was Africa's largest country by area until South Sudan split away in 2011.
- Gum arabic tapped from its acacia trees is used worldwide in soft drinks and candy.
- Camel caravans still cross its deserts along age-old trading routes.

### 🇹🇿 Tanzania
- Mount Kilimanjaro is the highest peak in Africa and the tallest free-standing mountain on Earth.
- The Serengeti hosts the great migration of millions of wildebeest and zebra.
- Ngorongoro Crater is a collapsed volcano teeming with wildlife.
- Olduvai Gorge is nicknamed the "Cradle of Mankind" for its hominid fossils.
- The Zanzibar archipelago was the heart of the old spice and Swahili trade.

### 🇹🇬 Togo
- It is a narrow sliver of a country, only about 100 km wide.
- French is its official language, a legacy of colonial rule.
- Lome, the capital, sits right on the Gulf of Guinea coast.
- It was a German colony called Togoland before being split by Britain and France.
- The Batammariba build fortress-like mud tower-houses at Koutammakou, a UNESCO site.
- Lome's Akodessewa fetish market is a famous hub of West African Vodun.
- Phosphate rock is one of its main mineral exports.
- Emmanuel Adebayor is its most famous footballer.
- The Gnassingbe family has ruled the country for over half a century.
- It stretches from a short Atlantic coast north to dry savanna hills.

### 🇹🇳 Tunisia
- The ruins of ancient Carthage, Rome's great rival, sit near Tunis.
- Its deserts doubled as the planet Tatooine in the Star Wars films.
- The amphitheater of El Jem is one of the best-preserved Roman coliseums anywhere.
- The Arab Spring uprisings began here in 2010.
- The troglodyte homes of Matmata are dug into the earth against desert heat.

### 🇺🇬 Uganda
- Bwindi's forests shelter roughly half the world's remaining mountain gorillas.
- Kidepo Valley, in the remote northeast, is one of Africa's wildest safari parks.
- Lake Bunyonyi, among Africa's deepest lakes, is dotted with green terraced islands.
- Murchison Falls squeezes the mighty Nile through a rocky gorge just seven metres wide.
- The ancient kingdom of Buganda lends the country both its name and a living monarchy.
- Idi Amin's brutal 1970s dictatorship remains one of the continent's darkest chapters.
- The Rolex, a rolled chapati wrapped around fried eggs, is a beloved street snack.
- Long-distance runner Joshua Cheptegei has shattered world records on the track.

### 🇿🇲 Zambia
- It shares the mighty Victoria Falls with Zimbabwe.
- Devil's Pool lets the brave swim at the very lip of the falls.
- Lake Kariba is one of the world's largest human-made reservoirs.
- The walking safari as a tourism experience was pioneered in South Luangwa.
- Copper mining has long been the engine of its economy.

### 🇿🇼 Zimbabwe
- Hyperinflation once forced it to print a 100-trillion-dollar banknote.
- The soapstone 'Zimbabwe Bird' from its ancient ruins appears on the national flag.
- It shares the thundering Victoria Falls, known locally as "the smoke that thunders."
- It takes its name from Great Zimbabwe, a vast medieval stone city built without mortar.
- Hwange National Park is famed for its enormous herds of elephants.
- Lake Kariba, one of the world's largest reservoirs, lies on its northern border.
- The balancing rocks of Matobo are natural granite sculptures.
- Robert Mugabe ruled for nearly four decades after independence in 1980.

## Asia (26)

### 🇦🇫 Afghanistan
- It was a key crossroads of the ancient Silk Road.
- Kabul, the capital, sits above 1,800 metres, ranking among the highest capital cities on Earth.
- Kabuli pulao, the national dish, layers rice with lamb, raisins, and slivers of carrot.
- Herat's soaring citadel and blue-tiled Friday Mosque anchor the country's cultured western reaches.
- Afghanistan has changed its national flag more times than any other country in the world.
- The elegant, long-haired Afghan hound is a dog breed rooted in these rugged mountains.
- The Panjshir Valley is celebrated for its emerald mines and its stubborn resistance fighters.
- Attan, a whirling group dance performed in a circle, is the national dance of Afghanistan.

### 🇦🇲 Armenia
- It was the first country in the world to adopt Christianity as its state religion, in 301 AD.
- Yerevan is older than Rome, founded in 782 BC.
- Mount Ararat, its national symbol, actually lies just across the border in Turkey.
- Carved stone crosses called khachkars are a unique national art form.
- Lake Sevan is one of the largest high-altitude freshwater lakes in the world.

### 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan
- It is nicknamed the "Land of Fire," partly for Yanar Dag, a hillside that has burned for centuries.
- It has more mud volcanoes than any other country on Earth.
- Baku's Flame Towers dominate the skyline of the Caspian Sea capital.
- Baku's old city sits below sea level, on the shore of the world's largest lake.
- The ancient Ateshgah fire temple drew Zoroastrian and Hindu worshippers.

### 🇧🇭 Bahrain
- It is an archipelago nation, its name meaning "two seas."
- The Tree of Life is a lone 400-year-old tree thriving alone in the desert.
- The ancient Dilmun civilization flourished here over 4,000 years ago.
- It hosts a Formula 1 Grand Prix at a purpose-built desert circuit.
- It was long famous for pearl diving before the age of oil.
- The King Fahd Causeway links the island nation by road to Saudi Arabia.
- Its Qal'at al-Bahrain fort mounds hold thousands of years of layered history.
- Manama, its capital, mixes glass skyscrapers with old souks and coral-stone houses.

### 🇧🇩 Bangladesh
- Bengali, its official language, is among the most widely spoken languages on Earth.
- The Sundarbans, Earth's largest mangrove forest, shelter the Royal Bengal tiger.
- It sits on the world's largest river delta, formed by the Ganges and Brahmaputra.
- It is one of the most densely populated countries on the planet.
- Cox's Bazar is one of the longest natural sea beaches in the world.
- It won its independence from Pakistan in 1971.
- It is one of the world's largest exporters of ready-made garments.
- Colourful cycle rickshaws swarm the streets of Dhaka.

### 🇧🇳 Brunei
- It is an oil-rich sultanate on the island of Borneo.
- The Istana Nurul Iman is one of the largest residential palaces in the world.
- Its citizens pay no income tax and receive free healthcare and education.
- Kampong Ayer is a centuries-old "water village" of stilt houses over the river.
- Much of the country is still covered in pristine tropical rainforest.

### 🇨🇾 Cyprus
- Greek myth names it the birthplace of Aphrodite, goddess of love.
- Its name is thought to be the root of the word "copper," mined here since antiquity.
- Its Troodos Mountains hide Byzantine churches painted with vivid medieval frescoes.
- The Karpas peninsula is a refuge for herds of free-roaming wild donkeys.
- It is the third-largest island in the Mediterranean, after Sicily and Sardinia.

### 🇬🇪 Georgia
- It has an 8,000-year winemaking tradition using buried clay vessels called qvevri.
- It sits in the Caucasus, straddling the boundary of Europe and Asia.
- Vardzia is a sprawling monastery city carved into a cliff face.
- It has its own unique alphabet, one of only a handful in active use.
- Tbilisi is named for its natural hot sulfur springs.

### 🇮🇷 Iran
- Persepolis was the ceremonial capital of the vast ancient Persian Empire.
- The Nasir al-Mulk "Pink Mosque" floods with rainbow light through stained glass.
- Ancient qanat tunnels have carried water beneath the desert for millennia.
- Naqsh-e Jahan Square in Isfahan is one of the largest public squares in the world.
- Desert towns use wind towers (badgirs) as an early form of air conditioning.

### 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan
- It is the largest landlocked country in the world, the size of Western Europe.
- The Baikonur Cosmodrome launched the first human, Yuri Gagarin, into space.
- Charyn Canyon is often likened to a smaller Grand Canyon.
- Its capital was rapidly built into a showcase of futuristic architecture.
- The "Golden Man," an ancient warrior in gilded armour, is a national treasure.

### 🇰🇼 Kuwait
- It recorded one of the highest reliable temperatures ever, around 54°C.
- Its vast oil reserves make it one of the richest nations per capita.
- It sits at the head of the Persian Gulf, bordering Iraq and Saudi Arabia.
- The Kuwaiti dinar is often the highest-valued currency unit in the world.
- Before oil, its economy rested on pearl diving and wooden dhow building.

### 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan
- Lake Issyk-Kul is the second-largest alpine lake in the world and never freezes.
- Nomadic culture endures with yurts and traditional eagle hunting.
- Roughly 90% of the country is mountainous, dominated by the Tian Shan.
- The epic of Manas is one of the longest oral poems ever recorded.
- Herders still drive livestock to high summer pastures called jailoos.

### 🇱🇦 Laos
- It is the most heavily bombed country per capita in history.
- The cool highlands of the Bolaven Plateau hide thundering waterfalls and fertile volcanic soil.
- Luang Prabang is a serene town of golden temples and morning alms rituals.
- The Plain of Jars is scattered with thousands of mysterious ancient stone urns.
- It is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia.
- The Mekong River runs almost the entire length of the country.
- The Kuang Si Falls tumble through tiers of turquoise limestone pools.
- It was once the kingdom of Lan Xang, "the Land of a Million Elephants."
- It is one of the world's few remaining one-party communist states.
- Sticky rice is the everyday staple, rolled into balls and eaten by hand.

### 🇱🇧 Lebanon
- Baalbek holds some of the largest and best-preserved Roman temples in the world.
- The cedar tree on its flag is a symbol dating back to biblical times.
- Byblos is among the oldest continuously inhabited cities on Earth.
- The Jeita Grotto is a vast cave system with a navigable underground river.
- The Phoenician alphabet, ancestor of ours, spread from its ancient coast.

### 🇲🇻 Maldives
- It is the world's lowest and flattest country, averaging just 1.5 metres above sea level.
- It is made up of around 1,200 coral islands grouped into ring-shaped atolls.
- Some beaches glow at night with bioluminescent plankton, a "sea of stars."
- It held the world's first underwater cabinet meeting to spotlight climate change.
- Overwater bungalows here helped define the modern luxury resort.

### 🇰🇵 North Korea
- It is one of the most isolated and secretive states in the world.
- It uses its own Juche calendar, counting years from its founder's birth.
- The Mass Games are among the largest choreographed performances on Earth.
- The unfinished, pyramid-shaped Ryugyong Hotel looms over Pyongyang.
- Mount Paektu, a volcano, is central to its national mythology.

### 🇴🇲 Oman
- Ancient falaj channels still irrigate villages after 2,000 years.
- It is the oldest independent state in the Arab world.
- Once a naval power, its empire reached down to Zanzibar off East Africa.
- The Grand Mosque in Muscat holds one of the world's largest handwoven carpets.
- Jebel Shams, the 'Mountain of the Sun,' overlooks a canyon dubbed Arabia's Grand Canyon.

### 🇵🇰 Pakistan
- It is home to K2, the second-highest mountain on Earth and among the deadliest to climb.
- The markhor, a wild goat with dramatic spiralling horns, is Pakistan's national animal.
- Islamabad's Faisal Mosque resembles a giant desert tent and ranks among the largest mosques worldwide.
- Malala Yousafzai, the youngest-ever Nobel Peace Prize laureate, was born in the green Swat Valley.
- The Khewra Salt Mine, one of the world's largest, yields the famous rosy pink Himalayan salt.
- Fragrant biryani, rice layered with spiced meat and saffron, is savoured across the country.
- The remote Hunza Valley is renowned for its apricot orchards and its residents' remarkable longevity.
- Cricket is a national passion, and Pakistan lifted the sport's World Cup trophy back in 1992.

### 🇵🇸 Palestine
- Bethlehem's Church of the Nativity marks the traditional birthplace of Jesus.
- Jericho is often cited as one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities on Earth.
- Its eastern edge touches the shore of the Dead Sea, the lowest place on land.
- Hisham's Palace preserves one of the finest early Islamic mosaic floors.
- Ancient olive trees and olive-oil making are central to its culture.

### 🇶🇦 Qatar
- It hosted the 2022 FIFA World Cup, the first in the Arab world.
- It is one of the wealthiest countries per capita, powered by natural gas.
- The Museum of Islamic Art is a landmark of modern desert architecture.
- It is a peninsula so flat its highest point is only about 100 metres.
- Falconry remains a treasured national pastime.

### 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia
- Mecca and Medina, Islam's holiest cities, draw millions of pilgrims each year.
- Hegra (Mada'in Salih) is a stunning Nabataean city cut into desert rock, like Petra.
- The Rub' al Khali, or "Empty Quarter," is the largest continuous sand desert on Earth.
- The "Edge of the World" is a dramatic cliff dropping onto an ancient seabed.
- It is building NEOM, a futuristic linear megacity in the desert.

### 🇰🇷 South Korea
- Its K-pop and drama "Hallyu wave" became a global cultural phenomenon.
- Jeju is a volcanic island with lava tubes and free-diving women called haenyeo.
- It has some of the fastest average internet speeds in the world.
- Gyeongbokgung is the grand royal palace at the heart of Seoul.
- Bathhouse spas called jjimjilbang are a beloved social ritual.

### 🇸🇾 Syria
- Damascus is among the oldest continuously inhabited capital cities on Earth.
- Palmyra was a dazzling desert caravan city ruled by Queen Zenobia.
- Krak des Chevaliers is one of the best-preserved Crusader castles anywhere.
- Aleppo's covered souks were among the largest ancient markets in the world.
- Ugarit produced one of the earliest known alphabets.

### 🇹🇯 Tajikistan
- The Pamir Mountains here are nicknamed the "Roof of the World."
- Roughly 93% of the country is mountainous.
- The Fann Mountains cradle dozens of jewel-colored alpine lakes.
- It holds some of the tallest dams in the world.
- The Pamir Highway is one of the highest and most remote road trips on Earth.

### 🇹🇱 Timor-Leste
- It is one of Asia's youngest nations, fully independent in 2002.
- It is one of only two majority-Catholic countries in Asia.
- The waters off Atauro Island are among the most biodiverse on the planet.
- Portuguese remains an official language from its colonial past.
- Its highland coffee is prized by specialty roasters worldwide.

### 🇾🇪 Yemen
- Socotra Island is famous for umbrella-shaped "dragon's blood" trees found nowhere else.
- Shibam is a walled city of mud-brick towers dubbed the "Manhattan of the Desert."
- Old Sana'a is filled with gingerbread-like tower houses centuries old.
- It is linked to the legendary Queen of Sheba.
- Honey from its remote highland valleys ranks among the most prized and costly in the world.

## Europe (24)

### 🇦🇱 Albania
- A paranoid Cold War regime dotted the country with around 170,000 concrete bunkers.
- Albanians call their country "Shqipëria," the "Land of the Eagles."
- It was the only European country whose Jewish population grew during the Holocaust.
- Mother Teresa, of Albanian heritage, is a national icon; the main airport bears her name.
- For decades under communism it was one of the most isolated countries on Earth.

### 🇦🇩 Andorra
- It is jointly ruled by the President of France and a Spanish bishop.
- It has no army and hasn't fought a war in centuries.
- It sits high in the Pyrenees and is a popular ski destination.
- It has one of the longest life expectancies in the world.
- Catalan is its official language, and duty-free shopping draws crowds.

### 🇧🇾 Belarus
- It preserves a strongly Soviet-era look in its monuments and architecture.
- The Struve Geodetic Arc, a pioneering survey of Earth's shape, left marked stations across Belarus.
- Nesvizh Castle, seat of the powerful Radziwiłł dynasty, is a UNESCO-listed baroque palace.
- Roughly 70 percent of the Chernobyl disaster's radioactive fallout drifted onto Belarusian soil.
- The national flag carries a red-and-white ornament borrowed from traditional woven cloth.
- Sprawling Minsk factories produce the powerful Belarus-brand tractors exported around the world.
- Kholodnik, a chilled beetroot soup, is served bright pink on sweltering summer days.
- Revolutionary hero Tadeusz Kościuszko was born on an estate in what is now Belarus.

### 🇧🇪 Belgium
- It is famous worldwide for its waffles, chocolate and over 1,000 varieties of beer.
- Brussels' Grand-Place is one of the most beautiful market squares in Europe.
- It is the birthplace of comic legends Tintin and the Smurfs.
- The Atomium is a giant model of an iron crystal built for a 1958 World's Fair.
- It once went a record 589 days without a formal government.

### 🇧🇦 Bosnia and Herzegovina
- The assassination that sparked World War I happened in Sarajevo.
- Sarajevo was among the first cities in Europe to run a full-time electric tram network.
- It has a tiny 20-km Adriatic coastline at the town of Neum, its only sea access.
- Cevapi, small grilled beef sausages served in flatbread, are a beloved national dish.
- Its three main peoples — Bosniaks, Serbs and Croats — share a rotating presidency.

### 🇧🇬 Bulgaria
- The Cyrillic alphabet was developed in the medieval Bulgarian Empire.
- Bulgarians traditionally nod their head to mean 'no' and shake it for 'yes.'
- Its yogurt is so prized that a beneficial bacterium is named after the country.
- Plovdiv is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in Europe.
- A haunting folk song from the country was sent into space aboard the Voyager probe.

### 🇪🇪 Estonia
- It is one of the most digital societies on Earth and invented e-residency.
- Around half the country is covered in forest.
- Tallinn has one of the best-preserved medieval old towns in Europe.
- Its "Singing Revolution" used mass choral gatherings to resist Soviet rule.
- It has thousands of islands and vast peat bogs to explore.

### 🇫🇮 Finland
- It is the "Land of a Thousand Lakes," and actually has around 188,000 of them.
- It has topped the world happiness rankings for years running.
- Sauna is a national institution, with more saunas than cars.
- Rovaniemi bills itself as the official hometown of Santa Claus.
- In the far north the sun never sets in summer and barely rises in winter.

### 🇽🇰 Kosovo
- It is one of the youngest countries in the world, declaring independence in 2008.
- It has one of the youngest populations in Europe.
- The medieval monastery of Visoki Dečani is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Rugova Canyon offers dramatic cliffs, caves and via ferrata routes.
- Pristina named a boulevard and raised a statue for Bill Clinton.

### 🇱🇻 Latvia
- Riga's Central Market fills a cluster of giant repurposed German Zeppelin hangars.
- Riga, its capital, is the largest city in the Baltic states.
- Riga's old town holds one of Europe's greatest concentrations of Art Nouveau architecture.
- Baltic amber, fossilized tree resin, has been prized along its coast for millennia.
- It regained its independence in 1991 as the Soviet Union collapsed.
- Its vast song festivals gather tens of thousands of singers at once.
- It is one of Europe's greenest countries, over half covered by forest.
- It joined the Baltic Way, a 600-km human chain of two million people in 1989.
- It is one of the three Baltic states, sitting between Estonia and Lithuania.
- It uses the euro and belongs to both the European Union and NATO.

### 🇱🇮 Liechtenstein
- It is one of only two doubly landlocked countries on Earth.
- It produces a huge share of the world's false teeth and dentures.
- You can rent the entire country for events, complete with a temporary logo.
- It has no airport and no military.
- The prince still lives in a castle perched above the capital, Vaduz.

### 🇱🇹 Lithuania
- The Hill of Crosses bristles with over 100,000 crosses left by pilgrims.
- Trakai Island Castle rises from a lake, a red-brick Gothic fortress of medieval dukes.
- Cepelinai, hearty potato dumplings shaped like zeppelins and stuffed with meat, are the national dish.
- Lithuanian ranks among the most archaic living tongues, strikingly close to ancient Sanskrit.
- Amber cast up along the Baltic shore has long been treasured as Lithuanian gold.
- Kaunas served as the temporary capital between the wars while Poland held Vilnius.
- A hidden Soviet missile base at Plokštinė now houses an eerie underground Cold War museum.
- Painter and composer Mikalojus Konstantinas Čiurlionis is revered as the nation's greatest artist.

### 🇱🇺 Luxembourg
- It is the world's only sovereign Grand Duchy, ruled by a Grand Duke.
- It made all public transport free nationwide, a world first.
- Its old city is honeycombed with defensive tunnels called casemates.
- It regularly ranks among the highest GDP-per-capita nations on Earth.
- Most residents speak Luxembourgish, French and German fluently.

### 🇲🇹 Malta
- Its megalithic temples are older than Stonehenge and the pyramids.
- It is the smallest and most densely populated country in the European Union.
- Maltese is the only Semitic language written in the Latin alphabet.
- The apostle Paul is said to have been shipwrecked on its shores.
- The Azure Window, a famous natural stone arch, collapsed into the sea in 2017.

### 🇲🇩 Moldova
- It is one of the least-visited countries in Europe.
- It is one of Europe's poorest countries.
- Milestii Mici holds the world's largest wine cellar, with millions of bottles.
- Wine is central to its identity, with vineyards covering much of the countryside.
- The breakaway region of Transnistria feels like a frozen Soviet time capsule.
- Romanian is its official language, and it shares deep ties with Romania.
- Landlocked between Romania and Ukraine, it has no coastline of its own.
- Orheiul Vechi is a cave monastery carved into a cliff above a winding river.
- It was part of the Soviet Union until it became independent in 1991.
- Russian is widely spoken alongside Romanian, a Soviet-era legacy.

### 🇲🇪 Montenegro
- Its name means "Black Mountain."
- The Tara River Canyon is the deepest gorge in Europe.
- The Bay of Kotor is a dramatic, fjord-like Adriatic inlet ringed by mountains.
- Its walled coastal towns and pebbly beaches draw Mediterranean tourists.
- It became independent from Serbia in 2006 after a peaceful referendum.
- It uses the euro as its currency despite not being in the European Union.
- Sveti Stefan is a tiny fortified islet turned exclusive resort.
- Durmitor National Park blends glacial lakes, peaks and deep pine forest.
- Podgorica is its capital, though historic Cetinje was the old royal seat.
- It is one of Europe's youngest and smallest countries.

### 🇲🇰 North Macedonia
- Lake Ohrid is one of the oldest and deepest lakes in Europe, full of unique species.
- Mother Teresa was born in its capital, Skopje.
- Skopje's centre is crowded with hundreds of statues and grand facades.
- Ohrid was once said to have 365 churches, one for each day.
- It shares a rich heritage tied to Alexander the Great's ancient Macedon.

### 🇷🇴 Romania
- Bran Castle in Transylvania is marketed as the home of Dracula.
- Bucharest's Palace of the Parliament is the heaviest building in the world.
- The Transfăgărășan is a spectacular serpentine mountain highway.
- The Danube Delta is one of Europe's greatest wildlife wetlands.
- Northern Moldavia has churches painted vividly inside and out.

### 🇷🇸 Serbia
- Inventor Nikola Tesla is a national hero, honoured at a Belgrade museum.
- The Roman emperor Constantine the Great was born in the city of Nis.
- The Guča trumpet festival draws vast crowds to a joyous frenzy of Balkan brass bands.
- Serbian is written fluidly in both Cyrillic and Latin alphabets in everyday life.
- Studenica Monastery, a UNESCO site, guards medieval frescoes within white marble churches.
- Slava, a family's yearly feast honouring its patron saint, is a cherished Serbian tradition.
- The looping Uvac Canyon curves below cliffs where rare griffon vultures nest.
- The northern city of Subotica dazzles with ornate Hungarian Art Nouveau architecture.

### 🇸🇰 Slovakia
- It has one of the highest numbers of castles and châteaux per capita in the world.
- It became independent in 1993 when Czechoslovakia peacefully split in two.
- It is one of the world's largest per-capita producers of cars.
- Its Carpathian wooden churches were built entirely without a single nail.
- It adopted the euro and joined the European Union in 2004.

### 🇸🇮 Slovenia
- Lake Bled has a tiny island church reached by traditional wooden boats.
- It is the only country with the word "love" hidden in its name.
- The Postojna and Škocjan caves are among the greatest cave systems in Europe.
- Its caves are home to the olm, a pale "baby dragon" salamander.
- More than half the country is covered in forest.

### 🇸🇪 Sweden
- It gave the world IKEA, ABBA, Spotify and the Nobel Prizes.
- Its coast has around 267,000 islands, more than any other country.
- The original ICEHOTEL is rebuilt from snow and ice every winter.
- "Allemansrätten," the right to roam, lets anyone hike and camp on open land.
- In Lapland the midnight sun and northern lights are seasonal spectacles.

### 🇨🇭 Switzerland
- The pyramid-shaped Matterhorn is one of the most photographed mountains on Earth.
- CERN's Large Hadron Collider circles underground beneath its border.
- It is renowned for chocolate, watches and cheese.
- It runs a system of direct democracy with frequent public referendums.
- The Red Cross was founded in Geneva, and its flag inverts the Swiss one.

### 🇺🇦 Ukraine
- It is the largest country lying entirely within Europe.
- Its fertile black soil made it a "breadbasket," a leading grain exporter.
- Kyiv is famed for its golden-domed Orthodox cathedrals.
- Chornobyl, site of the 1986 nuclear disaster, draws curious visitors.
- Intricately decorated pysanky eggs are a centuries-old folk art.

## Americas (27)

### 🇦🇬 Antigua and Barbuda
- The Devil's Bridge is a natural limestone arch carved by the pounding Atlantic.
- A vibrant summer Carnival celebrates the end of slavery with music and masquerade.
- Fungie and pepperpot, cornmeal dumplings served with a hearty spiced stew, is the national dish.
- The acclaimed novelist Jamaica Kincaid was born in the capital, St. John's.
- Its highest point, Mount Obama, was renamed in 2009 to honor the U.S. president.
- The tiny uninhabited island of Redonda is the nation's rarely visited third territory.
- Betty's Hope preserves the weathered windmill towers of a colonial-era sugar plantation.
- Benna, a lively calypso-like folk song, once spread news and gossip across the islands.

### 🇧🇸 Bahamas
- Wild pigs famously swim in the turquoise shallows of Exuma.
- Harbour Island is known for its blush-pink sand beaches.
- Columbus made his first New World landfall on one of its islands in 1492.
- Junkanoo, a dazzling costumed street parade, lights up its Christmas and New Year.
- Nassau, its capital, was once a notorious haven for Golden Age pirates.

### 🇧🇧 Barbados
- It is often credited as the birthplace of rum.
- It is the home island of pop superstar Rihanna, its national hero.
- Harrison's Cave is a dramatic network of underground limestone chambers.
- Flying fish are so iconic they appear on its coins and menus.
- Unlike its volcanic neighbours, it is built largely from coral limestone.

### 🇧🇿 Belize
- The Great Blue Hole is a giant, near-perfect circular sinkhole in the sea.
- It has the second-largest barrier reef in the world.
- The Maya city of Caracol once rivalled the greatest cities of its era.
- It runs the world's first jaguar preserve, at Cockscomb Basin.
- It is the only country in Central America with English as its official language.

### 🇨🇴 Colombia
- Caño Cristales is called the "river of five colors" when its plants bloom red.
- It is home to more bird species than any other country on Earth.
- Cartagena's walled old town is a jewel of Spanish colonial architecture.
- The Lost City (Ciudad Perdida) predates Machu Picchu by centuries.
- It touches both the Pacific and Caribbean, plus the Andes and Amazon.

### 🇨🇷 Costa Rica
- It abolished its army in 1948 and invested the savings in education and health.
- Despite its tiny size, it holds around 5% of the world's biodiversity.
- The Monteverde Cloud Forest drips with mist, orchids and quetzals.
- Its Nicoya Peninsula is one of the world's "Blue Zones" of longevity.
- "Pura vida" is both a national motto and a way of life.

### 🇨🇺 Cuba
- Its streets are full of lovingly maintained 1950s American cars.
- Havana's seaside Malecón is the city's living room and social heart.
- It is world-famous for hand-rolled cigars and son and salsa music.
- It is the largest island in the Caribbean.
- Old Havana is a UNESCO-listed treasury of colonial architecture.

### 🇩🇲 Dominica
- Sperm whales live in its waters year-round, a rarity anywhere on Earth.
- It is nicknamed the "Nature Island" for its wild, mountainous rainforest.
- Its Boiling Lake is one of the largest naturally boiling lakes on Earth.
- It has 365 rivers, said to be one for every day of the year.
- It is home to the Kalinago, the Caribbean's last surviving Indigenous people.
- The rare Sisserou parrot on its flag is found nowhere else on Earth.
- It is easily confused with the larger, Spanish-speaking Dominican Republic.
- Its rainforests have doubled as film sets for Hollywood pirate adventures.
- Its national dish, mountain chicken, is actually the meaty legs of a giant frog.
- Hurricane Maria devastated the island in 2017.

### 🇩🇴 Dominican Republic
- Santo Domingo is the oldest continuously inhabited European city in the Americas.
- It is the birthplace of the bachata and merengue music styles.
- Pico Duarte is the highest mountain in the Caribbean.
- Its amber has preserved insects for millions of years, inspiring "Jurassic Park."
- It is a powerhouse that produces a huge share of Major League Baseball players.

### 🇪🇨 Ecuador
- The Galápagos Islands inspired Darwin's theory of evolution.
- A monument marks where the equator ("middle of the world") crosses the land.
- Quito's colonial centre was among the first UNESCO World Heritage sites.
- Cotopaxi is one of the highest active volcanoes in the world.
- The "Panama hat" is actually woven in Ecuador.

### 🇸🇻 El Salvador
- It became the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender.
- It is nicknamed the "Land of Volcanoes."
- It is the smallest and most densely populated country in Central America.
- It is the only Central American nation without a Caribbean coastline.
- The pupusa, a stuffed griddled corn flatbread, is its beloved national dish.

### 🇬🇩 Grenada
- It is the "Spice Isle," a leading source of nutmeg — even pictured on its flag.
- An underwater sculpture park turns the seabed into an art gallery.
- Grand Anse is regularly named among the Caribbean's finest beaches.
- Its capital, St. George's, curves around a pretty horseshoe-shaped harbour of pastel buildings.
- The Grand Etang crater lake sits in the misty volcanic highlands.

### 🇬🇹 Guatemala
- Tikal's Maya temples rise dramatically above the rainforest canopy.
- Lake Atitlán, ringed by volcanoes, is often called the world's most beautiful lake.
- Antigua is a photogenic colonial city framed by smoking volcanoes.
- Semuc Champey is a staircase of turquoise limestone pools over a river.
- The market at Chichicastenango is one of the largest and most colourful in the Americas.

### 🇬🇾 Guyana
- Kaieteur Falls is one of the world's most powerful single-drop waterfalls.
- It is the only country in South America with English as its official language.
- Vast, near-untouched rainforest covers most of the country.
- Its rivers shelter giant otters and giant river turtles.
- Culturally it feels Caribbean, sharing a passion for cricket.

### 🇭🇹 Haiti
- It was once France's wealthiest colony, built on brutal slave-worked sugar plantations.
- Its name comes from an Indigenous Taino word meaning 'land of high mountains.'
- It became the first Black-led republic and first nation to permanently abolish slavery, in 1804.
- It shares the island of Hispaniola with the Dominican Republic.
- The mountaintop Citadelle Laferriere is the largest fortress in the Americas.
- Vodou is practised here as an official religion.
- A catastrophic 2010 earthquake devastated the capital, Port-au-Prince.
- Creole and French are its two official languages.
- Toussaint Louverture, a freed slave, led its revolution as a brilliant general.
- Its brightly painted 'tap-tap' buses and vivid naive-art paintings are famous.

### 🇭🇳 Honduras
- It was one of the original "banana republics."
- The Maya ruins of Copan are famed for their carved hieroglyphic stairway.
- Roatan and the Bay Islands sit on a world-class barrier reef.
- It is one of Central America's most mountainous countries.
- The baleada, a folded flour tortilla with beans and cream, is its beloved street food.
- A brief 1969 war with El Salvador became known as the "Football War."
- The lempira, its currency, is named for an Indigenous chief who resisted the Spanish.
- The Rio Platano rainforest is a vast UNESCO biosphere reserve.
- Tegucigalpa, its capital, sprawls across steep hillsides in a mountain bowl.
- Its name means 'depths' in Spanish, said to be coined by Columbus.

### 🇯🇲 Jamaica
- It is the birthplace of reggae and its global icon, Bob Marley.
- It produces sprinting legends like Usain Bolt from a tiny population.
- Jerk is its fiery signature style of spicing and grilling meat.
- It is the birthplace of the Rastafari movement.
- Visitors climb the terraced cascades of Dunn's River Falls.
- Its misty Blue Mountains rise steeply above the tropical coast.
- Ackee and saltfish is its national dish.
- Port Royal, once "the wickedest city on Earth," sank in a 1692 earthquake.
- It won independence from Britain in 1962, keeping the British monarch as head of state.
- Beach resorts around Montego Bay and Negril make tourism a mainstay.

### 🇳🇮 Nicaragua
- Ometepe is an island formed by two volcanoes rising from a freshwater lake.
- It is the largest country in Central America by area.
- A chain of smoking volcanoes runs down its Pacific side.
- Granada is one of the oldest colonial cities in the Americas.
- Lake Nicaragua was long famous for its rare freshwater sharks.
- The 1979 Sandinista revolution toppled the Somoza family dictatorship.
- Poet Ruben Dario, father of Spanish-language Modernismo, was born here.
- Gallo pinto, a mix of rice and beans, is the everyday national dish.
- Managua, on the shore of a lake of the same name, is its capital and largest city.
- Daniel Ortega, a former Sandinista rebel, has dominated its politics for decades.

### 🇵🇦 Panama
- The Panama Canal links the Atlantic and Pacific across the narrow isthmus.
- Because of its curve, you can watch the sun rise on the Pacific and set on the Atlantic.
- It forms a land bridge that let species migrate between two continents.
- The Guna people govern their own islands in the San Blas archipelago.
- Panama City's skyline is one of the most striking in Latin America.

### 🇵🇾 Paraguay
- The Indigenous Guaraní language is co-official and spoken by most people.
- The Itaipú Dam was for years the largest hydropower plant in the world.
- Its flag is the only national flag with different designs on each side.
- The Jesuit mission ruins are a haunting UNESCO World Heritage site.
- The wild Gran Chaco is one of South America's last great wildernesses.

### 🇰🇳 Saint Kitts and Nevis
- It is the smallest sovereign nation in the Americas by area and population.
- Founding father Alexander Hamilton was born on the island of Nevis.
- The hilltop Brimstone Hill Fortress is a UNESCO "Gibraltar of the West Indies."
- Its two lush volcanic islands are joined by a narrow strait called The Narrows.
- A scenic railway once built for sugar cane now carries sightseers.
- It was among the first Caribbean islands Europeans colonized.
- Mount Liamuiga is a dormant volcano crowned by a crater-lake rainforest.
- It runs one of the world's oldest citizenship-by-investment programs.
- Basseterre, on St. Kitts, is the nation's capital and main port.
- Its economy long ran on sugar cane before shifting to tourism.

### 🇱🇨 Saint Lucia
- The twin Pitons are dramatic volcanic spires rising straight from the sea.
- You can drive right up to the edge of its bubbling "drive-in volcano."
- It is said to be the only country named after a woman.
- It has produced two Nobel laureates from a tiny population.
- Sulphur springs let visitors bathe in warm, mineral-rich mud.

### 🇻🇨 Saint Vincent and the Grenadines
- Its lush scenery starred in the "Pirates of the Caribbean" films.
- The active La Soufrière volcano dominates the main island.
- The Tobago Cays are a postcard-perfect sailing and snorkeling paradise.
- Bequia keeps a rare traditional whaling heritage alive.
- Its volcanic origin gives many beaches striking black sand.

### 🇸🇷 Suriname
- It is the most forested country on Earth, over 90% covered in jungle.
- Its people blend Indian, Javanese, African Maroon, Creole and Indigenous roots.
- It is the smallest sovereign country in South America.
- Dutch is its official language, a legacy of Dutch colonial rule.
- Paramaribo's wooden Dutch colonial old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- It gained its independence from the Netherlands in 1975.
- Giant leatherback turtles nest on its remote Atlantic beaches.
- Many stars of the Dutch national football team trace their roots here.
- In its capital, Paramaribo, a mosque and a synagogue famously stand side by side.
- It sits on South America's northern Atlantic coast, between Guyana and French Guiana.

### 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago
- Columbus reached the island in 1498 and named it for the Holy Trinity.
- It is the birthplace of the steelpan, calypso and soca music.
- Its Carnival is one of the biggest and most spectacular in the world.
- It is the southernmost Caribbean nation, lying just off the coast of Venezuela.
- Pitch Lake is the largest natural deposit of asphalt on Earth.
- Flocks of scarlet ibis paint the Caroni swamp red at sunset.
- Its economy runs largely on oil and natural gas.
- Both the limbo dance and Angostura bitters originated here.

### 🇺🇾 Uruguay
- Almost all of its electricity comes from renewable sources.
- It has more cattle than people, several times over.
- Jose Mujica, the frugal ex-president, gave away most of his salary to charity.
- The chivito, a towering steak sandwich, is Uruguay's unofficial national dish.
- Candombe, an Afro-Uruguayan drumming tradition, powers the world's longest Carnival.
- La Cumparsita, the most famous tango ever written, was composed in Montevideo.
- Colonia del Sacramento's cobbled Portuguese quarter is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- The remote village of Cabo Polonio has no roads, running water, or power grid.

### 🇻🇪 Venezuela
- Angel Falls is the tallest uninterrupted waterfall in the world.
- It has produced a record number of Miss Universe and beauty-pageant winners.
- Independence hero Simon Bolivar, called the Liberator, was born in Caracas in 1783.
- Pabellon criollo, layering shredded beef, rice, beans and plantain, is the national dish.
- Explorers named the land Little Venice for stilt houses raised over Lake Maracaibo.
- Conductor Gustavo Dudamel rose to fame through El Sistema, its famed youth music program.
- The Merida cable car climbs higher than any other cableway in the world.
- Capybaras, caimans and anacondas thrive across the vast Llanos grasslands.

## Oceania (10)

### 🇫🇯 Fiji
- It is an archipelago of over 330 islands scattered across the South Pacific.
- Its waters are the "soft coral capital of the world" for divers.
- The ceremonial drinking of kava is central to its social life.
- Traditional firewalking on hot stones is practiced on the island of Beqa.
- Cloudbreak is one of the most famous and challenging surf breaks on Earth.

### 🇰🇮 Kiribati
- It is the only country to straddle all four hemispheres at once.
- Its Line Islands are among the first places on Earth to greet each new day.
- It contains one of the world's largest marine protected areas.
- Its low-lying atolls are on the front line of rising sea levels.
- It spans an ocean area larger than India, yet its land is tiny.

### 🇲🇭 Marshall Islands
- Bikini Atoll's sunken warships, from nuclear tests, are a diver's time capsule.
- Its sailors navigated using ingenious "stick charts" reading ocean swells.
- It created one of the largest shark sanctuaries in the world.
- Kwajalein has one of the largest lagoons on the planet.
- It is made up of low coral atolls barely above the waves.

### 🇫🇲 Micronesia
- Nan Madol is an ancient stone city built on artificial islets, the "Venice of the Pacific."
- Chuuk Lagoon holds one of the world's greatest concentrations of WWII shipwrecks.
- Some of its marine lakes teem with harmless golden jellyfish.
- It is spread across roughly 600 islands over a vast stretch of ocean.
- Traditional stone money discs are still valued on the island of Yap.

### 🇳🇷 Nauru
- It is the smallest island nation and the smallest republic in the world.
- Phosphate mining once briefly made it one of the richest places per capita.
- It is one of the few countries with no official capital city.
- Decades of mining left much of its interior a moonscape of rock pinnacles.
- You can drive all the way around the island in under half an hour.

### 🇳🇿 New Zealand
- Its landscapes doubled as Middle-earth in the "Lord of the Rings" films.
- The Māori haka is a powerful ceremonial dance known worldwide from rugby.
- It has several times as many sheep as people.
- Milford Sound is a breathtaking fiord of sheer cliffs and waterfalls.
- Rotorua bubbles with geysers, hot springs and steaming mud pools.

### 🇼🇸 Samoa
- It lies in the heart of Polynesia in the South Pacific.
- Traditional tatau tattooing is applied by hand with tools used for centuries.
- It is considered one of the cradles of Polynesian culture.
- Fa'a Samoa, "the Samoan way," orders daily life around family and village chiefs.
- In 2011 it skipped a whole day, jumping west across the international date line.
- Author Robert Louis Stevenson spent his final years and is buried here.
- The To Sua ocean trench is a stunning swimming hole reached by a tall ladder.
- It has produced many rugby stars and shares heritage with actor Dwayne Johnson.

### 🇸🇧 Solomon Islands
- Some Melanesians here are born with naturally blond hair.
- Guadalcanal was the scene of pivotal, brutal World War II battles.
- Marovo Lagoon is one of the largest saltwater lagoons in the world.
- Kavachi is a rarely seen underwater volcano nicknamed "Sharkcano."
- Its warm waters hide countless dive-worthy wartime wrecks.

### 🇹🇴 Tonga
- It is the only Pacific island nation never formally colonized, and still a kingdom.
- Visitors can swim alongside humpback whales in its warm waters.
- The Haʻamonga ʻa Maui is a mysterious ancient stone trilithon.
- A 2022 undersea volcanic eruption here was one of the most powerful ever recorded.
- It once led the world as the first place to ring in each New Year.

### 🇻🇺 Vanuatu
- Mount Yasur is one of the most accessible active volcanoes in the world.
- Its land-diving ritual, jumping with vines, inspired modern bungee jumping.
- It has repeatedly ranked among the "happiest" countries on Earth.
- The wreck of the SS President Coolidge is a famous, easily reached dive.
- Some villagers venerate Britain's Prince Philip in a unique movement.
