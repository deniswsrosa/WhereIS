# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-24_

**Where in the World is Clara San Diego? (Enhanced remake)** ("the app") is a single-player,
offline-first game. Your privacy is simple to describe because the developer does not collect
personal data or operate a game server.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app collects no personal or sensitive information. It has no account system,
no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and it does not contain advertising.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Game saves** (your detective name, rank, cases solved, and the case in progress) are stored
  **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. They never leave the device and are not
  transmitted anywhere.
- Deleting the app removes these saves. A previously purchased World Campaign may be restored
  from the Google account's Play purchase record after reinstalling.

## Network use

The app does not require a network connection for gameplay. It connects to Google Play Billing to
retrieve the optional World Campaign offer, start a purchase you request, and restore ownership.
Google Play handles the transaction under Google's terms; the app never receives card or bank
details and sends purchase data to no developer server.

When **you** tap **Share** on a result screen, the app hands a short, spoiler-free text summary to
Android's system share sheet so you can choose another app. Clara does not transmit that summary.

## Children

The app contains no ads or data collection. It offers one optional, non-consumable World Campaign
purchase through Google Play and no subscriptions, loot boxes, or virtual currency. See
`PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md` for the Google Play declarations.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the repository owner via the project's GitHub page.
