# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-29_

**Where in the World is Clara San Diego? (Enhanced remake)** ("the app") is a single-player,
offline game. Your privacy is simple to describe because the app is built to need nothing from you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app collects no personal or sensitive information. It has no account system,
no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and it does not contain advertising.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Game saves** (your detective name, rank, cases solved, and the case in progress) are stored
  **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. They never leave the device and are not
  transmitted anywhere.
- Deleting the app removes these saves.

## Network use

The app does not require a network connection to play. The only time it touches another app is
when **you** tap **Share** on a result screen: the app hands a short, spoiler-free text summary to
Android's system share sheet so you can post it if you choose. The app itself sends nothing.

## Children

The app is suitable for children and contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no data
collection. See `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md` for the Google Play Families declarations.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the repository owner via the project's GitHub page.
