# Privacy Policy — Where is Clara San Diego?

_Last updated: August 6, 2026_

**Where is Clara San Diego?** ("the app") is an offline, single-player game.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in exists.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
- The app makes no network requests during play.

## Data stored on your device

Game progress (careers, settings, language choice) is saved **only on your device**, in the
app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Notifications

If you enable Reminders in the game's Options menu, the app schedules **local**
notifications on your device. No notification data leaves the device, and reminders can be
turned off at any time in the same menu or in Android's system settings.

## Purchases

An optional in-app purchase unlocks additional game content. Payment is handled entirely by
Google Play; the app never sees or stores your payment details.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages, contains no ads, no chat, no external links, and collects
no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if online features are added), the updated version
will be published at this same address before the change takes effect.
