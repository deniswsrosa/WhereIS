# WhereIS — Welcome Cards (sample of 10)

Draft arrival/welcome cards for the expansion. The game names the **country** (e.g. "Fly to Vanuatu"), not a city, unless a city is world-famous. Each card = **3 clues** + bonus **flag** and **money** clues + a **say-hello** line (usually learning; tagged _hint_ only when the language obviously reveals the country).

_Flags, currencies and greetings web-verified 2026-07-30. Mockup: the "Welcome Cards — Review" artifact._

## ✈ Vanuatu  🇻🇺
*the South Pacific (Oceania)*

**Clues**
1. You've landed on a chain of about 80 volcanic islands scattered across the South Pacific.
2. Climb Mount Yasur and you can peer straight down into one of the world's most active volcanoes.
3. Long ago, islanders here leapt from tall wooden towers with vines tied to their ankles — the world copied the idea and called it bungee jumping.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A red-and-green flag with a black triangle at the mast; a yellow Y-shape crosses it, and inside the triangle a yellow boar's tusk curls around two crossed fern leaves.
- 💰 **Money:** People pay with the vatu.
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: Most people speak Bislama, a playful cousin of English — say hello with “halo” (HAH-loh).

## ✈ Kiribati  🇰🇮
*the central Pacific (Oceania)*

**Clues**
1. This is the only country that sits in all four hemispheres at once — north, south, east and west.
2. Its far-eastern Line Islands are among the very first places on Earth to see each new sunrise.
3. Its 33 low, sandy atolls are sprinkled across a patch of ocean larger than India, yet its land is tiny.

- 🏴 **Flag:** The top half is red with a golden frigatebird gliding over a golden rising sun; the bottom half is blue with three wavy white stripes for the ocean.
- 💰 **Money:** There's no money of its own — people here spend the Australian dollar.  _(shared — weak clue)_
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: In the local language, Gilbertese, hello is “Mauri” (MAH-oo-ree).

## ✈ Botswana  🇧🇼
*Southern Africa*

**Clues**
1. Each year, floodwaters turn the Okavango Delta into a watery maze where elephants, lions and hippos gather.
2. More elephants roam here than anywhere else on Earth — over 130,000 of them.
3. Diamonds found underground helped turn it from one of Africa's poorest nations into one of its quiet success stories.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A light-blue flag crossed by a black stripe with thin white borders — the blue stands for rain, the black-and-white for its people living side by side.
- 💰 **Money:** The money is the pula — a word that also means “rain,” the most precious thing in a dry land.
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: In Setswana you greet someone with “Dumela” (doo-MEH-lah).

## ✈ Madagascar  🇲🇬
*the Indian Ocean, off East Africa*

**Clues**
1. This giant island broke away from Africa around 160 million years ago and has drifted on its own ever since.
2. Because of that, about 9 in 10 of its animals — including every wild lemur on Earth — live nowhere else.
3. Along the famous Avenue of the Baobabs, ancient trees stand like fat, upside-down giants.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A white stripe down the mast, then two wide bands — red on top, green on the bottom.
- 💰 **Money:** People count money called the ariary.
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: The language is Malagasy — a friendly hello is “Salama” (sah-LAH-mah).

## ✈ Namibia  🇳🇦
*Southern Africa*

**Clues**
1. You've reached the edge of the Namib, thought to be the oldest desert on the planet.
2. At Sossusvlei, rust-red sand dunes rise over 300 metres — among the tallest anywhere.
3. Its foggy Atlantic shore, littered with old shipwrecks and whale bones, is called the Skeleton Coast.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A bold red diagonal edged in white splits the flag; a blue triangle with a golden sun sits at the top-left, and a green triangle at the bottom-right.
- 💰 **Money:** The Namibian dollar is the local money — and South Africa's rand is accepted too.
- 📝 _Fun fact for a hello: its official language is actually English, though many people speak Oshiwambo, Afrikaans or German. (No everyday greeting shown — the local hellos are tricky to pin down; want me to add one?)_

## ✈ Oman  🇴🇲
*the Arabian Peninsula (Middle East)*

**Clues**
1. You're in the oldest independent country in the Arab world, on the southeast corner of the Arabian Peninsula.
2. For thousands of years, precious frankincense was carried from here across deserts and seas.
3. Long ago its sailors built a trading empire that reached all the way down to Zanzibar, off the coast of Africa.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A red band down the mast holds a white emblem — a curved khanjar dagger crossed over two swords — beside three stripes of white, red and green.
- 💰 **Money:** People pay with the rial (careful — several nearby countries use a coin with a similar name).  _(shared — weak clue)_
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: Arabic is spoken here — a warm, everyday hello is “marhaba” (MAR-ha-ba).

## ✈ Kazakhstan  🇰🇿
*Central Asia*

**Clues**
1. This is the largest landlocked country on Earth — as wide as all of Western Europe — with endless grassy steppe.
2. From its Baikonur Cosmodrome, the first human ever, Yuri Gagarin, blasted into space.
3. Its brand-new capital was raised from the bare steppe into a city of gleaming, futuristic towers.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A sky-blue flag with a golden sun and a soaring golden eagle in the middle, and a band of golden pattern running down the mast.
- 💰 **Money:** The money is called the tenge.
- 👋 **Hello** _(hint)_: People speak Kazakh, where hello is “Salem” (sah-LEM) — the language even shares the country's name.

## ✈ Montenegro  🇲🇪
*the Balkans (Southern Europe)*

**Clues**
1. Its very name means “Black Mountain,” and steep dark peaks tumble right down to the sea.
2. The Bay of Kotor curls deep inland like a fjord, ringed by mountains and old walled towns.
3. Slicing through its north is the Tara River Canyon — the deepest gorge in all of Europe.

- 🏴 **Flag:** A red flag with a golden border and a golden two-headed eagle in the center, a small lion on its chest.
- 💰 **Money:** It spends the euro — even though it isn't part of the European Union.  _(shared — weak clue)_
- 👋 **Hello** _(learning)_: In Montenegrin, say hello with “Zdravo” (ZDRAH-voh).

## ✈ Nicaragua  🇳🇮
*Central America*

**Clues**
1. It's the biggest country in Central America, with a chain of smoking volcanoes running down its Pacific side.
2. On a huge freshwater lake sits Ometepe, an island built from two volcanoes joined together.
3. Granada, on the lake's shore, is one of the oldest colonial cities in all the Americas.

- 🏴 **Flag:** Three bands — blue, white, blue — with a badge in the middle showing five volcanoes, a rainbow and a red liberty cap.
- 💰 **Money:** People count money called the córdoba.
- 👋 **Hello** _(hint)_: Like much of Latin America, people here speak Spanish — say “Hola” (OH-lah).

## ✈ Guyana  🇬🇾
*the north coast of South America*

**Clues**
1. Almost the entire country is wrapped in thick, near-untouched rainforest.
2. Deep in that jungle, Kaieteur Falls thunders down in one of the world's mightiest single drops.
3. Though it sits on mainland South America, it feels Caribbean — and it is absolutely cricket-mad.

- 🏴 **Flag:** The 'Golden Arrowhead' — a green flag with a long golden arrow edged in white and a red triangle edged in black, all pointing across from the mast.
- 💰 **Money:** The money is the Guyanese dollar.
- 👋 **Hello** _(hint)_: Here's a surprise: it's the only country in South America where the official language is English — so just say “Hello.”
